How the cross-prediction methods actually perform over 25 breeding cycles

A new-user run driving nextgenCrossDesign as a breeder would — through ng_run_cross_prediction, evolution optimizer throughout, trained on the phenotyped parents. Thirteen method/metric/optimizer arms raced against SimpleMating and a random control. The story is a gain-versus-diversity trade-off: the arms that win long-term are the ones that spend genetic variance slowest.

The two things that matter

Gain climbs; genetic diversity drains

Left: cumulative genetic gain per cycle (higher is better). Right: expected heterozygosity — the genetic variance still available to select on (falling toward zero is the danger). Read them together: the arms highest on the left at cycle 25 are the ones that stayed highest on the right.

var_simple wins the long game. The weakest single-generation metric is the strongest recurrent performer: as a relationship-distance criterion it favours unrelated parents, preserves variance, and so keeps compounding gain while the pure-usefulness metrics exhaust their variance and plateau. The strategy dial reaches nearly the same place by managing diversity explicitly. SimpleMating finishes last, with the most depleted diversity.

Optimizer axis · metric held at usefulness (PMV)

Evolution matches exact MIP — and both beat greedy

Same cross-scoring metric, three optimizers. In a one-shot objective bake-off MIP wins, but on realized gain over 25 cycles evolution is level with MIP (7.20 ± 0.30 vs 7.07 ± 0.44 — the difference is within noise) while greedy trails at 6.78. Since evolution also scales where MIP is size/time-guarded, it is the sound default.

Method. AlphaSimR simulates founders → trait → RIL lines (the truth); each cycle ng_run_cross_prediction trains ridge marker effects on the phenotyped parents and predicts crosses among them; progeny advance by single-seed descent (F2→F6); the best 30 recycle as next-cycle parents. 5 chromosomes, ~200 QTL, ~1000 SNP, h²=0.5; OCS diversity penalties λ_group=0.05, λ_mating=0.02. Residual-het RIL loci imputed to the major homozygote for the inbred kernel. SimpleMating invoked externally via the package's validated wrappers.
